## First lecture: inspiration

1. **Cai Guo-Qiang's cAI™, "A Manifesto: 10 principles of coexistence between humans and AI"** (WePresent). The artist's custom AI, trained on his body of work and writings, writes a manifesto on how humans and AI can coexist.
   - https://wepresent.wetransfer.com/stories/manifesto-cai-guo-qiang-ai
   - Poster download: https://we.tl/t-z6HTv1bo7L
2. **Andrea Lipps, "Data as Artistic Medium: A Curatorial Framework."** Talk, Data|Art 2025.
   - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_MkHsWw0gb8
3. **Ryoji Ikeda, *data-verse 1*.** Antonino La Vela, "Ryoji Ikeda at the Venice Biennale: When Data Stops Being Neutral," *The Art Trace*, July 10, 2026. Open-source scientific datasets turned into sound and light. The essay argues a dataset is already a decision about what gets counted. The work premiered at the 2019 Biennale.
   - https://www.thearttrace.com/2026/07/ryoji-ikeda-venice-biennale-data-stops-being-neutral.html
4. **"Build with AI at The Met."** Brett Renfer, The Met, May 1, 2026. A one-day hackathon (April 16, 2026) on The Met's Asian art collection. Highlighted projects: *Instrument Explorer*, *Fish The Met*, *Semantic Explorer*. Sponsored by Anthropic.
   - https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/build-with-ai-at-the-met
5. **Peiqi Su, *The Penis Wall* (2014).** Benjamin Sutton, *Artnet News*, May 23, 2014. NYU ITP thesis: 81 motorized 3D-printed forms driven by gallery visitors' movement or live NYSE stock prices. Mention the subject to the class before showing images.
   - https://news.artnet.com/art-world/wall-of-wiggling-3-d-printed-penises-is-actually-good-26370
   - Video: https://player.vimeo.com/video/95494620
6. **Richard The / TheGreenEyl.** Portfolio: https://richardthe.com
   - a. *Ground Truth* (Eyebeam, with Frédéric Eyl): large-format prints of computer-vision training datasets (faces, fingerprints, hand gestures). https://richardthe.com/projects/GroundTruth
   - b. *Necto* (Venice Architecture Biennale 2025, with SO – IL and Mariana Popescu): 3D-knitted natural fibers with a DNA-encoded coating that stores data in the material. https://richardthe.com/projects/Necto
   - c. *Universal Music Data Sculptures* (Berlin, 2015): three 8-meter flip-dot friezes showing live artist tweets. https://thegreeneyl.com/universal-music-data-sculptures
   - d. *Liquid Archive* (MIT Museum): ML builds an endless collage from the museum's collection. https://richardthe.com/projects/LiquidArchive
   - e. *Found in Translation* (21_21 Design Sight, Tokyo, with Google Creative Lab): spoken sentences machine-translated across 24 languages. https://richardthe.com/projects/FoundInTranslation
   - f. *Step Into The Web* (Mozilla, Berlin): live websites broken into HTML parts. https://thegreeneyl.com/step-web
   - g. *Data Center Mural Project* (Google Creative Lab): murals on the buildings behind "the cloud." https://richardthe.com/projects/DataCenterMuralProject
   - h. *Bodies in Motion* (Milan Design Week 2019, for Humanscale): 15 motorized light beams trace visitors' bodies. https://richardthe.com/projects/BodiesInMotion
   - i. *Unresolved Image* (Eyebeam, with Frédéric Eyl): a print that changes with viewing distance. https://richardthe.com/projects/UnresolvedImage
